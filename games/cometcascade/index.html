COMET CASCADE

  • Every comet costs fuel in stardust. Tap LAUNCH (or press Space) and it falls through the star field into an orbit pod.
  • Each pod multiplies your fuel. Edge pods are rare and pay the most.
  • Calm keeps things steady, Nebula swings wider and Supernova puts everything on the edges. More rows means more bounces and bigger edge pods.
  • Fly comets to climb the ranks. Every new rank drops a stardust bonus.
